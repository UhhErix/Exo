Before you install.

Requirements
Windows 11 only, 64-bit. Setup stops on older Windows. Installs for your Windows user only, with no administrator rights.
The installer
It isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: verify the SHA-256 above, then choose More info and Run anyway.
Captures
In-repo native Exo Capture records instant replay, clips and snips with your GPU hardware encoder (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel) plus Liquid Glass FPS readout.
Exo account
Setup asks you to create an Exo account or sign in after Welcome so preferences, collections and play time sync across your Windows 11 PCs. Paths, GPU/display and Discord settings stay on this PC.
Backups
Automatic daily backups to Documents\Exo\Backups\Launcher keeping 30 days. Reinstalls offer one-click Restore on first run.
Updates & Safety
Updates and store tools come only from updates.getexo.app with SHA-256 verification. Pending updates wait until no game is running.
Changing upscaler files in a game with anti-cheat can risk that game account. Exo detects anti-cheat from disk, asks per game and never touches Riot games. Privacy details.
