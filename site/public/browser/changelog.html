Exo Browser changelog

Exo Browser is archived. Explore the current Exo apps.

On your Windows 11 PC: getexo.app

1.4.6 — November 6, 2026

  • Updates come only from Exo's own address. Exo Browser gets its updates from updates.getexo.app and no longer asks GitHub for them, not even as a fallback. Each update download is checked against its size and SHA-256.

1.4.5 — November 5, 2026

  • Exo Launcher's look, current. The Bar, Search, menus, Settings and every sheet now draw from Exo Launcher 3.24.2's own design (the copy here had stayed at an older one): Geist instead of Inter, Exo's tokens, Liquid Glass, controls, notices and motion. The copy refreshes only from a Launcher checkout's committed design.
  • Settings has a sidebar of pages, as Exo Launcher's does: General, Search, Look, Spaces, Privacy, Extensions, Bookmarks, History, Downloads, Import and About, each under its title with a close at the top right and its own action (New space, Full history) on the title line. Before, it was one long column with no title or close. Search and the menus still open it at a page or a setting.
  • Boosts, Zap and Mods are labs, off by default (Settings > About > Labs): a site's own CSS typed as code, picking things off a page, and themes and tweaks of the browser itself weren't finished. Off, their page, rows, menu items and commands are gone and nothing of theirs applies; what you set is kept for when you turn one on.
  • Tabs across the top share the strip evenly. Each took its title's width, so a long title stayed wide while the short ones beside it went down to one letter.
  • Down the side, New tab is a row like the tabs under it instead of a lone + in the middle of the pane, and Exo Vault's sheet has a title, a close and its Show and Lock on the title line, as a Settings page.
  • The new tab page names a site at a number or one word whole: 127.0.0.1's tile read "0", bbc.co.uk's "Co".
  • No 5-second freeze on a new profile's first start. The first tab was made at the same moment as the Bar's own view, and the window then waited for the engine for 5 s in about half of first starts. The tabs now come right after the Bar's view.
  • Freeze reports say where the window waited (the code it waited in, by module), and the Bar's page reports only what actually held it, not the wait for its first paint, which logged most starts as a hang.
  • tools/layout-check.mjs starts its own test copy on the second screen, checks every prompt (Ctrl+Tab, a page's dialogs, permissions, Zap, the status line, a page's menu, the error card) and a matrix of 1280, 1600 and 1920 wide at 100%, 125% and 150% page scale.
  • tools/smoke.mjs hooks the Bar's page once it is its own document, not the blank one before it, which made the click-on-the-address check throw now and then. _Checked: the failure seen once on this build (window.__sent missing); the fix not yet run end to end._
  • Typing right away. When Exo Browser opens, and on every new tab (Ctrl+T, the + button, a closed tab leaving a new tab), the address field has the keyboard with its caret, without a click; a click on the page or the Bar still goes there, and a page that loads after you started typing doesn't take your keys.
  • No more frozen tabs because of another tab. Every tab of a site shared one engine process, so a page waiting on its own alert in a tab you couldn't see froze the tab you were using, with no way to click. Each tab has its own process now, a dialog from a tab off screen is answered Cancel as in Chrome, and one left open in a tab you leave is too.
  • A page that stops responding says so. Its card offers Try again, which starts it afresh; before, it stayed frozen.
  • Search opens with one clean edge. Clicking the address grew Search in while the frost under it was already full size, which drew a doubled outline and shadow; it opens at once now.
  • Search suggestions as you type from your search engine, under your own tabs, bookmarks and history, never for an address or in a private tab; Settings > Search turns them off. Search engines open clean results in Windows' language and region (Google's Web results without AI Overview), with a short line about each in Settings > Search, and addresses are told from searches by real domains (example.com, nas:5000, localhost; not file.txt).
  • Profiles are gone: no profile button in the Bar and no profile pane (it could only show the Exo Profile; signing in never worked there). Nothing of yours moves.
  • The interface is 290 KB of script and styles smaller (1 MB with the fonts) without the profile pane, and the Bar's page starts listening before it says it's ready, so nothing the window says at start is lost.
  • A freeze or a crash leaves a trace, and a report. The window's thread and the Bar's page are watched: anything holding them over 200 ms is logged with what ran, and a crash, a hang over 5 s or the Bar's process failing sends Exo a short report stripped of anything personal (the same error once a day, five a day). The last run's log stays as exo-previous.log. PRIVACY.md and Help say what is sent.
  • Live folders whose feed address has a query (?format=rss) update again: Windows' fetches went out without it.
  • Updates' GitHub fallback works again: only the first host listed for updates was allowed, so GitHub was always refused.
  • Newest interface tools: Vite 8.3.6, Tailwind 4.3.4 and the rest; WebView2's SDK stays at 1.0.4314.35, the newest stable.

1.4.4 — November 5, 2026

  • A loaded page is never covered by "Can't reach". The card over a tab now follows only that tab's main document: it shows when that navigation failed and none of its own page came in, and never for a navigation a newer one replaced, one that turned into a download, or a certificate error of a script or picture from another host (those used to be counted against the page). The card also fills exactly the page's place, so it no longer leaves a strip of the page showing or covers the Bar.
  • Exo Vault stays out of the way. An icon sits inside the sign-in field you click or Tab into, and a compact dropdown opens right under it: your logins for the site (the first few, the rest by a search), a strong password on a sign-up form, a 2FA code. A field the page focuses on its own at load shows nothing, and nothing opens on load or navigation. Esc or the dropdown's close keeps it away for that site until Exo Browser closes (the icon still opens it on a click); "Not now" on the save offer does the same for that site. A save is offered only after a sign-in you submitted, never a page's script.

1.4.3 — October 31, 2026

  • A page on screen takes clicks again. Putting a tab to sleep could finish in the moment you switched to it, and the page then drew but ignored every click until Exo Browser was closed. Coming back to a tab now resumes it, and a sleep that finishes on the tab you're looking at is undone.
  • Reopening starts clean. Closing hid the window and kept the single-instance lock while it saved and closed its pages, so opening it again during that handed the new start to a window that was leaving and then exited. A start during close now waits for the previous one to let go and opens on its own, and the close log says how long the save and the pages took.

1.4.2 — October 31, 2026

  • Bugs fixed.
  • A page's own connect or sign-in window opens while protection starts. A page that opens its own window (Factory's connect flow, a sign-in) had that window's first page held for Exo Shield like any other, and the window could never open, showing "Can't reach …" instead. Its first page isn't held now; Exo Shield covers every page after it as before.
  • The update mirror is part of the release. tools\Publish-Updates.ps1 uploads the installer, its checksum and What's New to the update mirror and only then points the newest-version answer at them, so an update can't be offered before its files are there (1.4.1's mirror step was missed).

1.4.1 — October 31, 2026

  • Protection before the first page. Pages wait until Exo Shield is ready, including private tabs and Little Exo. If protection takes longer, Exo says "Protection is starting" and lets you choose "Open anyway" instead of silently loading without it.
  • A new profile shares Shield's files. On the same drive, Exo Shield uses hard links to the installed files instead of copying them; where a link cannot be made, it copies the file as before.
  • New tab is ready before you ask. Exo Browser keeps one new tab page made and hidden, so New tab shows it at once and makes the next one a few seconds later: from the command to the page on screen took 174 ms (1.35 s at worst) and now takes 27 ms (100 ms at worst). It also no longer pulls the window in front of another app when a tab opens in the background. None is kept while you play or on a PC short of memory.
  • Notifications are off, with no setting. Sites can't show notifications, ask to, or sign up for push messages: the engine has no notification API at all, as Safari on iPhone has none, so no site can nag for them either. A site you allowed them before loses it; other site permissions stay as you set them. Settings › Privacy › Site permissions no longer lists notifications.
  • Settings is wider, 900 instead of 720, the same width as Exo Launcher's.
  • Glass that reads cleanly over any page. Menus and sheets (Settings, Exo Vault, What's New) frost the page under them more deeply: a photo or a diagram under Settings showed as a sharp block of its colors beside the switches, and now it's only a soft wash of them.
  • Exo's opening. Exo Browser opens as Exo Launcher does: its mark settles in on the window's color and holds while what you see first loads behind it (the Bar, the page on screen and its fonts, not every image after them), then sinks back and fades as the window comes up under it, whole. A site that never finishes holds it 3 seconds at most. With Reduce motion on, there's no motion.
  • Light while you play or stream, by itself. While a game runs or OBS, Streamlabs, XSplit or the like is open, Exo Browser behind it steps back: lower priority and Windows' efficiency mode for all of its processes, its memory handed back 15 seconds after it goes behind (588 MB to 29 MB on a new tab), and update checks and live folders wait. It's back at full speed the moment you bring it to the front, and a tab playing sound never steps back, so music or a call never crackles. Exo Launcher tells it when; without Exo Launcher it looks for itself. While streaming software is open, Streamer Mode is on: Exo Vault's usernames, emails and details show as dots, and your Exo Profile shows how you signed in instead of your email.
  • On a PC with Windows' power throttling turned off (some tweak tools turn it off), Windows has no efficiency mode to give, so stepping back says so once in Exo Browser's log ("unavailable on this PC") and stops asking for it. Lower priority and handing memory back work as before.
  • Disk priority steps back too. Behind a game, Exo Browser's processes also read and write the disk at low priority, so its cache and history writes never queue ahead of the game's loading. A download coming in keeps normal priority, and everything is back as it was when Exo Browser comes to the front.
  • A fast mouse costs far less. A page draws a hover once per frame of your screen, but a high-rate mouse (8,000 Hz) sends over a hundred moves a frame, and Exo Browser passed each one on. Now a page gets at most one move a frame, the latest, from your screen's own refresh rate; a drag, a drawing, a selection and the first move into a page still get every point, and a click or a scroll never comes before the move just ahead of it. Moving an 8,000 Hz mouse over a page for a while used 34.3 seconds of processor time instead of 51.8.
  • A tab put to sleep lets go of its picture first. As a tab's page is closed to save memory, Exo Browser takes its picture out of the window and lets go of it before the engine closes the page, so nothing on Exo's side still holds what the engine frees.
  • Exo Shield on today's HaGeZi list: 214,202 domains.
  • You, at the top right. A person in a circle beside Settings opens your Exo Profile right in the window, under the Bar, and closes it again, as Exo Launcher's Profile room does: no tab of its own. It's only the icon, never your picture or a video, so the Bar has nothing to load or play for it.
  • Your whole Exo Profile. The profile built into Exo Browser is the one Exo Launcher shows: your look, banner, layout, stats, gear and moving pictures, not only your name and picture. It opens whole in one fade, its motion keeps playing while the window is on screen, and your profile's files are read from this PC for Exo's own pages only: no website can tell what's there.
  • What a button did shows on the button. Import, Export and Clear in Settings, and Bring in on first run, say what they did on their own row, as Exo Launcher's do, instead of a notice in the corner too. Notices are for what happens on its own: downloads, updates, an extension the store page's own button added.
  • Bookmark cards are clear glass. On the new tab page each bookmark is a clear card inside Exo's thin edge with the site's icon and name, instead of a tile washed in the site's colors (or a made-up gradient for a site with no icon). The quote over them is gone. The Bookmarks choice in Settings › New tab works as before.
  • Downloads show Exo's bar. The Downloads button shows how far your downloads are with Exo's thin bar along its foot instead of a ring around its arrow, and the bar glides while a download doesn't say its size.
  • The accent is a tint, not a control's color. A chosen accent tints the tab and space on screen, the selected text and the address's loading light. A switch that's on stays Exo's green, the primary button stays white and the keyboard's focus ring stays white, as in Exo Launcher.
  • Bugs fixed.
  • Your profile no longer locks the window. With the Bar hiding by itself, opening your profile covered the whole window, the Bar and the side tabs included, so nothing but Escape (which nothing said) got you out. The profile now opens under the Bar and beside the side tabs, both stay shown over it, and Profile closes it again, a tab takes you away, and Escape still works.
  • Your profile shows what Exo Launcher shows. Its banner, background and moving picture were missing in Exo Browser: they were read from this PC but never handed to the page.
  • In the smallest window, with tabs across the top, the Bar's right end ran off the window (Close past its edge) and the spaces and tabs shrank to slivers. The Tools capsule and the window's buttons never give way now: under 600 pixels the address and the tabs share what's left, the tabs show the one on screen, and the space on screen shows its icon without its name.
  • With several tabs across the top, the space on screen was cut to its first letter ("H…"). The spaces keep their width and the tabs give way.
  • With tabs down the side, the new tab page sat off center, as it centered itself in the whole window instead of the space beside the tabs. It centers in what you see now.
  • The tab strip, the side tabs and Settings' column were cut off hard at their edges with more beyond. They fade into the edge where there's more, as Exo Launcher's lists do.
  • With many tabs squeezed, a tab playing sound drew its sound icon over the site's icon. The sound button takes the icon's place now, so Mute is still a click away; the same on a pinned tab.
  • Find in page squeezed its field to a sliver for a big count ("1284 of 1000000"). Counts read "1,284 of 9,999+" now, and the field keeps room to type.
  • The Downloads menu showed only a percentage, "0%" for a download that doesn't say its size, and Failed like any other state. It shows "2.1 of 40 GB" (how much has arrived when the size isn't known, with a gliding bar), and Failed in red.
  • In a page's right-click menu, rows that can't be done (Back with nowhere to go) looked like the rest, and the first row lit up as the menu opened. They're dimmed and the arrow keys skip them, and a row is lit at the start only when the keyboard opened the menu.
  • Grey lines explaining a setting were back: in Exo Vault (the dice, the master password, the empty list, a login's Name), Settings (Default browser, Import, Material) and first run (Bring in, Make default), and under a page that couldn't be reached while offline. They're gone. Your own CSS's note about an unmatched brace is an error, so it shows in red under the field, only while the braces don't match.
  • Removing a bookmark, a history page, an archived tab, a site search or a link route, and removing or cancelling a download, happened at the first click with no undo. Each asks on the button now, as Delete does elsewhere.
  • Adding an extension from Settings, or with Install on the Chrome Web Store, answered with a notice in the corner. Settings' Add says "Added …" on its row (an error in red under it) and is off while its field is empty; the Bar's Install reads Adding, then Added, or Not added in red. The store page's own button still answers with a notice.
  • Exo Vault's values were 16 pixels in a monospace font, with spaced-out codes and hidden passwords. They're 13 pixels in Inter with even-width figures, the Note field has a card's corners and doesn't resize, and an item's Back sits before its title.
  • On first run in the smallest window, the rows ran under Back and Continue and were cut off. The column scrolls above the buttons now.
  • In a short window, a Settings choice's menu opened up over the Bar, and with tabs down the side a notice lay over them. The menu stays under the Bar and scrolls, and a notice is never wider than the room beside the side tabs.
  • A page that never loaded showed a lock in the address. A page that couldn't be reached shows no lock.
  • Placeholders in the address and Find were dimmer than Exo's, Arabic and other right-to-left tab and bookmark titles were cut from the wrong end, side tab titles stopped short of the close button's room even while it was hidden, "Links from other apps" and the first bookmark had no line above them, "Add to Favorites" read in title case, and the CSS fields could be dragged taller. All fixed.
  • A tab's menu with tabs down the side stopped at 520 pixels, half an item showing, with the window's height to spare. Long menus show whole now, and scroll only in a window too short for them.
  • A notice at the top right lay over Peek's buttons while Peek was open, their words showing through it. It sits under Peek's bar now.

1.4.0 — October 29, 2026

Every question a website asks is Exo's own now, confirms happen on the button you pressed, Settings and Exo Vault lose their sidebars, and Exo Browser reads each PC's memory, screens and battery right. Every number here was measured on the same PC.

  • What sites ask, in Exo's own look. Edge drew a site's questions in its own boxes; now Exo does, in its glass and outside the page, naming the site that really asked. A page's alert, confirm, prompt and Leave site? are Exo's dialog ("example.com says"), and a page that asks three times in ten seconds gets no more until it opens another page. Camera, microphone, location, notifications, the clipboard and the rest ask under the address, Block first; your choice is kept for the site, and Settings › Privacy › Site permissions lists it with Reset. A notification request a page makes as it loads, with no click from you, is refused without asking. A link to another app asks first with the app's real name ("example.com wants to open Steam"), and link types used to run programs from a page never open. A site's sign-in box (a router's) is Exo's too. A page that can't be reached shows Exo's card with what went wrong and Try again, and a certificate that isn't valid adds Continue anyway, asked on the button.
  • Confirms happen on the button. Clear and Delete no longer open a dialog: the button you pressed becomes the question in red ("Clear and sign out?", "Clear 12 tabs?", "Delete it and 3 tabs?"), and a second press does it. Escape, a click elsewhere or four seconds put it back. Trash icons grow into the question.
  • Settings in one column, and Search finds every setting. No section list and no grey lines explaining a setting: one column of sections in a narrower sheet. Type a setting's name or what it's about in Search ("dark", "font", "pip") and Settings opens at it, lit for a moment with its switch ready. Calm icons is Gray site icons.
  • Exo Vault, simpler. No sidebar: its views (logins, cards, password health, the trash, import and export, its settings) are a menu beside Lock, and its grey notes are gone. Password health shows how many logins each problem holds.
  • Fits your PC. A PC whose built-in graphics keep part of its memory is no longer counted a size smaller: a 16 GB PC with them on closed background tabs after an hour, as an 8 GB one; now after two. The first window is sized for your display scaling (on a 4K screen at 200% it was a quarter of the screen). A window that was on a monitor you've unplugged or changed opens wholly on a screen. On battery or with Energy Saver on, tabs you left stop running after five minutes instead of fifteen.
  • The engine's built-in AI is off for sites. WebView2 let pages reach its Summarizer, Translator and language detector, which hung any site that used them and gave sites one more way to tell PCs apart. Sites now take the path they have for Firefox and Safari. The privacy policy also says that a site's voice typing sends what your microphone hears to Microsoft once you allow the microphone.
  • Exo Shield on today's HaGeZi list: 211,671 domains.
  • Exo Browser's source isn't published any more. Its GitHub page keeps the releases, the docs and the privacy policy; Exo Shield's source stays public, as its license wants.
  • Bugs fixed.
  • Under Windows' contrast themes, the tab strip, side tabs, Search's list, the vault's fill menu and the new tab page kept their scrollbars hidden. They show now, with their room kept.
  • A silent install (/S) opened Exo Browser even with /NOLAUNCH, and Setup dropped /DESKTOP= and /STATUSFILE= the same way. They reach the install now.

1.3.1 — October 28, 2026

Two fixes: your bookmarks are on the first new tab after an update, and a bookmark shows a site’s current icon, not the one it had when you first saved it.

  • Bugs fixed.
  • The first start after an update could open on a new tab without your bookmarks, or with parts of a page missing. The update brings a new Exo Shield, and Exo Browser adds it again as it starts. What pages asked for in that moment failed, and the new tab never asked again. On such a start, pages now wait until Exo Shield is in, about a second; on any other start nothing waits. The new tab also asks again if its first try fails.
  • A bookmark kept a site's old icon after the site changed it: getexo.app's showed Exo Launcher's logo instead of Exo's. A site's large icon was fetched once and kept for good. It's now checked as you visit the site, once a session: fetched again when the page names a different icon or the one kept is over a week old, and dropped when the page offers none, so the site's own small icon shows.

1.3.0 — October 28, 2026

Updates that find you as the window opens, Exo Shield’s count beside the address, tabs that give their memory back a minute after you leave them, Windows 11’s scrollbars, and a batch of fixes. Every number here was measured on the same PC.

  • Update available, as the window opens. When a newer Exo Browser is out, the window opens on one small sheet with its version and Update now. Update now downloads it with its progress, checks it, installs it and opens it again. Its changes show in What's New once the new version opens. A release that comes out while the window is open waits for the next open, and Settings › About shows it meanwhile. Offline, nothing shows. A download that fails says why, with Try again and Not now. If the last update didn't take, and Exo Browser opened again on the same version, the sheet says so from the start, with Not now beside Try again, so it never goes round in circles. Copy logs, beside any failure, copies a short report to paste into an issue: the versions, the error and the log's last lines.
  • What's New and the update sheet hold the window. While either is up, nothing behind it scrolls, takes a click or answers a shortcut. Only the window's own buttons and Alt+F4 still work. A press outside What's New no longer closes it; Continue or Escape does.
  • A start in the background stays there. A start that asks not to come to the front no longer takes it: the window brought itself to the front as it set up its frame. Neither sheet takes the keyboard until the window is activated.
  • What Exo Shield blocked, beside the address. A shield and a number by the star show how many requests Exo Shield stopped on the page you're on: ads, trackers, malware and the scripts it swaps for harmless stand-ins. The number counts afresh on every new page. Exceptions, header changes and tracking parameters taken off a link don't count, since they block nothing.
  • Scrollbars as Windows 11 draws them. Pages' scrollbars are thin, lie over the page instead of taking a strip beside it, and show while you scroll or point at them, in the page's own light or dark. Windows' contrast themes keep the classic ones.
  • Reduce motion is the switch's alone. Exo Browser's own Bar, sheets and new tab no longer go still because Windows' animation effects are off while Reduce motion is off. A first start sets the switch from Windows' animation effects; from then on only the switch decides, as in every Exo app. Pages keep seeing Windows' own choice.
  • First run, brought up to date. The welcome says Exo Shield blocks ads and trackers from the first page. Your look now has Reduce motion, set from Windows' animation effects as you start. Exo Vault's ready line says it locks after your time and when Windows locks, not when you close the browser. No step takes the keyboard until the window is active.
  • Settings › About shows an update's progress beside its words. "Downloading 1.3.0 · 54%" sits with Exo's bar, not a bar inside the button, and the line no longer says updates come from GitHub. Setup's screens wait for their window to be active before taking the keyboard too.
  • Tabs you leave give back their memory after a minute. A tab you haven't looked at for a minute now lowers its memory on every PC, not after 10 minutes as on a PC with memory to spare. Coming back to it still takes less than a frame. Tabs playing sound, tabs you keep, and tabs with a download in progress are left as they are. Freezing and closing tabs you've left still wait as long as before: frozen, a tab took noticeably longer to come back. _Measured on a throwaway profile with eight sites: a minute and a half after leaving them, Exo Browser held 1 GB in use instead of 2.4 GB (1.3 GB private, from 1.8). Coming back to a lowered tab took about 15 ms the first time, 10 ms for one still awake, and 7 ms after that. A frozen one took 16 to 36 ms._
  • Windows' Installed apps names Exo, as for every Exo app. Exo Browser's entry lists Exo as its publisher, links to getexo.app/browser for its page and updates, and to the Exo Discord for help. Its files say Exo too, with the copyright still Erix (UhhErix)'s. Each start puts right the entry's version, publisher and links if they're out of date, so an entry left on an older version catches up, as yours had on 1.1.7. It touches only the entry Setup made for the folder it runs from, and never creates one.
  • Bugs fixed.
  • In a narrow window with tabs down the side, a tab's menu could lie over the tabs, where tab names could show through its glass. When there isn't room beside the tabs, the menu now narrows, and its longest items end in "…".
  • An update to Exo Shield could leave the old version running. WebView2 kept the worker it was first added with, so a new build is now added again, once.
  • Split view with a sleeping tab showed a black half for about a quarter of a second while that tab drew its page. The tab now draws out of sight at its pane's size, and the split opens once it has its page.
  • Reload the moment a sleeping tab woke left it blank for good. It reloaded the blank page the tab starts on, cancelling its own page; a blank tab's Reload now opens its page.
  • Glass over the top of a page was half see-through, so the page under it read sharp. It showed in Search's first row and at the top of a menu.
  • A new tab's Most visited row repeated sites already in your bookmarks, and named sites by their address ("Bbcgoo…", "Theverge"). It now leaves those out and uses each site's own name, such as Good Food, The Verge and The GitHub Blog.

1.2.0 — October 28, 2026

A pass over every line of Exo Browser: faster where you feel it, lighter on memory and the processor, safer in Exo Vault, one look and motion with Exo, and a dozen bugs gone. Every number here was measured on the same PC before and after.

  • Faster where you feel it. A new tab shows its page in 34 ms (286 before), and Settings opens in 12 ms the first time (300). Search's first open drops no frames (one of 49 ms before), and Exo Vault unlocks in 237 ms (300).
  • Lighter. The graphics process holds 140 MB at rest (364 before). Minimizing pauses every pane of a split view and Peek, not only the tab in front. A page that retitles itself (an unread count) no longer rewrites your saved tabs every 1.5 s. Spinners and the loading sweep pause while another app, a game, is in front. Exo Vault's keeper sleeps instead of waking every 2 s, using no processor time over 20 s.
  • The first start after an update is quick. Setup now refreshes Exo Shield's 51 MB of lists while it installs. The browser used to do it on its first start, before anything showed, which made that start 5.2 s.
  • Exo Vault, harder to read. What you type into a password field no longer appears in the page's markup. After you change the master password, the old one opens nothing left on this PC. Copies of a password, or of the whole vault for an export, no longer stay in memory after use, and hidden fields show 12 dots whatever their length.
  • Asks before it can't be undone. Deleting a space, clearing history or the archive, clearing cookies, deleting a vault item forever and emptying the vault's trash ask first.
  • Private stays private. A private tab's title no longer reaches your history. Clearing history also removes the site icons kept for sites nothing else uses.
  • Updates from Exo's own address, with GitHub behind it when it doesn't answer, so moving the downloads can never strand an installed copy again.
  • Bugs fixed.
  • Tabs opened with Ctrl+click or a middle click could be archived within 30 s.
  • Closing a pane of a split view left a blank half, and panes beside the tab in front could read as asleep.
  • A private tab could take extensions, imported sign-ins and the color scheme for itself.
  • A paused download kept a timer running, and a background tab whose page crashed reloaded at once instead of when shown.
  • One trackpad swipe ran through every space, the What's New list showed a missing icon, and the toolbar command always said Hide.
  • Dragging to reorder in Settings snapped back, and the glow when a page finished loading never showed.
  • One look and motion with Exo. Every transition runs on Exo's times and curves, menus grow from their button, and what the keyboard opens lands at once. Notices last Exo's notice time and wait while you point at them, and grey notes that only explained are gone.
  • The newest parts. It's now type-checked with TypeScript 7.1 beta (0.4 s instead of 2.7), and Argon2 runs its SSE2 build, with the same keys and about 20% faster.
