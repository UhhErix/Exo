Help

Short answers for Exo Launcher and Exo Cinema, plus Discord and direct contact with Erix.

Launcher

  • Install. Get Exo Launcher from the download page. Needs 64-bit Windows 11; installs per-user with no admin rights.
  • Updates. Offered automatically on startup when idle from updates.getexo.app with SHA-256 verification; waits until running games exit.
  • Exo account & recovery. Syncs preferences and play time across PCs. Keep your recovery code somewhere safe — it is shown once.
  • Where data lives. Library and settings in %LocalAppData%\ExoLauncher, notes in Documents\Exo Notes, backups in Documents\Exo\Backups\Launcher, captures in Videos.
  • PC > Apps & Shaders. Uninstall unwanted bloat cleanly, inspect startup tags, and manage precompiled shader caches in PC > Shaders.

Cinema

  • Install. Get Exo Cinema from the download page for 64-bit Windows 11.
  • Jellyfin only. Home, Movies, Shows and playback come directly from the Jellyfin server you sign in to.
  • Updates. Settings > About > Check for updates checks Exo’s update mirror and installs compact file diffs.
  • Where data lives. Caches and DPAPI-sealed server credentials stay in %LocalAppData%\ExoCinema on your PC.
  • Diagnostics. Crash, freeze and playback failure reports are scrubbed of addresses and titles before leaving; view what is sent in Settings > About.
