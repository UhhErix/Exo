How it plays.

Direct Jellyfin playback
Home, Movies, Shows, Continue Watching and Next Up come strictly from your Jellyfin server. Files play direct without transcoding loss.
Native libmpv engine
Hardware decoding, HDR and Dolby Vision tone mapping for your monitor, and automatic frame-rate matching.
Sharper pictures
On AC power with a dedicated GPU, ArtCNN sharpens anime and NVIDIA RTX Video Super Resolution upscales SD/HD video, pausing whenever a game runs.
Keyboard shortcuts
Space or K pauses, Left/Right seek, J/L jump 10 seconds, F toggles fullscreen and M mutes, plus Skip Intro and Up Next.
Sealed credentials
Server credentials are sealed with Windows DPAPI per Windows user and never leave your machine to third parties.
Privacy & updates
Nothing you watch is ever sent to Exo. Delta updates download only changed files from updates.getexo.app after SHA-256 verification. Privacy page.
