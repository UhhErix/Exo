getexo.app: three directions, phase 1. Each frame is a 1440 px desktop home hero, one product section (Launcher, Library), the nav and the footer. The copy is identical in all three and comes from design/copy.md. Screenshots are placeholders until the capture plan in design/imagery.md is approved.

Brand rules in every frame: Void black (#000), Liquid Glass for everything that sits on the page, Geist, Tabler outline icons, no stock photos, no testimonials, no counts, no third-party scripts.

A. Quiet Library. Centred, one column, very large type (120 px headline, 72 px section). Motion: the picture rises once as it enters the view, nothing else moves. Calm and editorial; the product is the page.
